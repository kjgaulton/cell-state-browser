Genes to programs to states

Gene
The basic unit of activity, a gene encodes for a protein that performs specific biological function in a cell.
Program
A group of genes that have coordinated expression or activity, due to shared regulation and potential involvement in similar biological processes.
State
The overall expression phenotype of a cell, defined by the combined activity levels of different programs.

States and disease are closely linked: a cell can shift into a particular state as a consequence of disease, or adopting a particular state can itself drive disease. Programs relate to biological pathways in a similar way, since a pathway is a process that produces a functional outcome in a cell, and a program captures the coordinated genes that carry that pathway out.
